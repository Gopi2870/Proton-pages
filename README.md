# Proton Pages

> AI-Powered Chemistry Intelligence, Education, and Virtual Laboratory Platform

Proton Pages is a next-generation scientific workbench and pedagogical interface engineered for research chemists, educators, and university students navigating molecular structures, reaction simulations, and AI-driven chemical synthesis.

## Core Modules

- **Instrument Deck & Dashboard**: Central research console with daily briefings, streak tracking, active simulations, and instrument launchers.
- **Interactive Periodic Table**: Full 118-element interactive explorer with orbital configurations, periodic trends, and spectral classifications.
- **Molecular Explorer**: Real-time 2D/3D molecular conformations, electrostatic surface projections, ligand informatics, and isomer libraries.
- **Reaction Engine**: Matrix inversion reaction balancing, stoichiometric yield calculations, thermodynamic state functions ($\Delta H^\circ, \Delta S^\circ, \Delta G^\circ, K_{eq}$), and atom conservation verification.
- **Virtual Chemistry Lab**: Apparatus rig simulating burettes, flasks, pH meters, conductivity probes, and dropwise acid-base titrations with real-time curve plotting.
- **AI Chemistry Tutor**: Dedicated chemical co-pilot with step-by-step mechanism breakdowns, electron arrow tracing, and concept discussions.
- **Learn & Courses**: Structured academic modules, Newman/Fischer stereochemistry visualizers, and interactive course navigation.
- **Practice & Quizzes**: Mechanistic assessment suite featuring transition state geometry diagrams, hints, and scoring.
- **Teacher & Institution Portal**: Cohort analytics, student mastery distribution, simulation volume tracking, and intervention queues.

## Technology Stack

- **Framework**: React 18 / 19 + TypeScript
- **Build Tool**: Vite
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS configured with the Stitch Scientific Design System tokens
- **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter, JetBrains Mono
- **Icons**: Google Material Symbols Outlined
